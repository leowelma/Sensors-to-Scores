# Ethics, Privacy, and Security

## Ethics Approval

This study was approved by the `Ethics Review Board of the Faculty of Social & Behavioural Sciences` of Utrecht University.  
**Approval number: FETC-25-2042**

## Data Privacy

No personal data were collected in the course of this research. All analyses were performed on the publicly available UAH-DriveSet, which was collected and published by the University of Alcalá under their own ethical framework.

The dataset contains no personally identifiable information. Participants are identified only by anonymised driver codes (D1-D6). No de-anonymisation was attempted or is possible from the public release.

## Informed Consent

No informed consent was required as no new data were collected. The UAH-DriveSet was used under its original terms of public availability for academic research purposes.

## Security

No sensitive data were used or stored in this project. All data used are publicly accessible at:  
http://www.robesafe.uah.es/personal/eduardo.romera/uah-driveset/

## Anonymisation

The UAH-DriveSet uses anonymised driver identifiers (D1-D6). No demographic or personally identifiable information is included in the dataset beyond age and gender, which are reported only at the group level in the thesis.
